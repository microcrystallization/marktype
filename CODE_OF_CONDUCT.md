# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in the MarkType community, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and software quality.

Project maintainers reserve the right to moderate comments, issues, or pull requests that do not align with these standards.
